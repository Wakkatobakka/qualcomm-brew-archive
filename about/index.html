About the archive

Wakkatobakka maintains this independent reference for BREW software and the people who made it.

Collection

The 104 records come from the Qualcomm BREW Games collection. Its two subsets contain 84 RAR packages under brew2010 and 20 ZIP packages under brew2018. The folder names are the collector’s groupings. A game may have more than one package, including a separate display build.

Each SHA-256 identifies the exact nested archive as cataloged. The inventory shows member filenames and their recorded display sizes. The original collection was checked against all 104 recorded fingerprints. Where handset descriptors contain titles, credit strings or versions, those values appear under Files & versions. Handset compatibility and playability require separate evidence.

Editorial method

Research starts with the original label, member paths and any edition clues. Period reviews, release announcements, developer accounts and surviving images are checked against that evidence. A source can establish who made a game, which build a reviewer played or what a carrier offered. Claims about a specific preserved build need a link to its own package evidence.

Where a title or edition is uncertain, the entry names the clue and what remains to be found. Quotes are given in the speaker’s original wording, with English translations when needed. Image captions identify the material, its origin and the edition it depicts.

Credits & corrections

Preservation credit goes to the original collectors and contributors to the Internet Archive item. Scans, period photographs and screenshots carry their own source credits. Copyright remains with the respective rights holders. The site is an independent archive.

To send a correction, open a GitHub issue with the entry and a supporting source.
