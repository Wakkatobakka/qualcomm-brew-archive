MilitaryMadness2

Hudson named Military Madness 2 as the feature-phone predecessor of its iPhone strategy game.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosFiles & versionsSources

The game

Military Madness grew out of Hudson’s 1989 Nectaris, a turn-based battle for bases on the Moon. When Hudson brought the sequel to the iPhone as Military Madness: Neo Nectaris, it explicitly named Military Madness 2 on traditional mobile devices as the game it had adapted.[1][2]

Development

Hudson had already made the first game for Japanese BREW handsets in 2004. It put 16 maps and 23 unit types on KDDI’s EZ Apps service at 210 yen per download. That year a separate Vodafone edition adjusted command entry for phones and offered extra eight-stage map packs. The 2004 reports describe the first Nectaris. The later iPhone listing describes a different branch, with new touch controls and a new score.[2][3][1]
