Camera Phone Darts

GoSub60 used the phone’s camera to turn the handset itself into a darts controller.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

You aimed a dart by moving the phone. GestureTek co-founder Francis MacDougall also described a mode where you photographed friends, put their pictures on a wall and threw tomatoes and other objects at them.[1]

Development

GestureTek made three camera-based motion trackers for BREW: Shake, Rock and Roll. Its Roll tracker turned changes in the camera image into inputs that played like a joystick, provided there was enough light and detail to track. The archive contains eyemocamera and eyemorocknroll modules alongside a game executable named gs60cpd.mod. Those names tie it closely to the game and middleware MacDougall described.[1][2]

People & studios

GoSub60

Release & reception

In December 2006 MacDougall said GoSub60 planned to publish Camera Darts over the Qualcomm network in the following month. The interview gives an announcement window. A dated carrier listing would establish when the game actually became available.[1]
