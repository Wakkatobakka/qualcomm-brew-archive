City ID

Cequint charged a monthly fee to put a city and state next to an unfamiliar number.[1][2][3]

In this entry

The applicationPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The application

City ID displayed the city and state where an incoming number was registered. Cequint’s lookup covered more than 20,000 North American cities and towns. The Seattle startup sold the result as an extra piece of information when a caller was missing from the phone’s address book.[1][2][3]

People & studios

Cequint

Developer of the caller-ID service (Commercial City ID)[1]

Alltel

U.S. carrier distributor (LG AX275 rollout announced 2007)[1]

Verizon Wireless

U.S. carrier distributor (Initial LG Voyager offering in 2008)[3]

Release & reception

In May 2007 Alltel announced that it would preload the app on the LG AX275. The service had a 15-day trial, followed by a subscription at around two dollars a month. Verizon introduced it on the LG Voyager in September 2008 and now lists City ID among its retired services.[1][2][3][4]
