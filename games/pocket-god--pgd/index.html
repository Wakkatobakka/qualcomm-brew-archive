Associated credits: Bolt Creative — original game

Pocket God began at Bolt Creative as a small, rapidly updated iPhone experiment by Dave Castelnuovo and Allan Dye.

In a 2010 interview, Castelnuovo discussed plans to expand the title to Java and BREW along with other platforms. The existence of this package is separate from establishing the exact date and developer of the BREW adaptation.
