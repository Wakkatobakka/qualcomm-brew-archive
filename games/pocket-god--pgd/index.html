Pocket God

Bolt Creative’s island sandbox reached Verizon’s BREW feature phones in September 2010.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

On Pocket God’s island, you can lift the inhabitants into the air, strike them with lightning or set off the volcano. Capcom described those interactions when it announced the Verizon feature-phone release.[1]

Development

Dave Castelnuovo and Allan Dye came up with the original iPhone game over the 2008 Christmas holidays. They kept extending it with updates after launch. By July 2010 Castelnuovo was talking about Java and BREW versions with publishing partners, alongside an iPad spin-off and a comic book.[2]

Bight Games adapted Pocket God for Verizon’s range of handsets. Allan Dye noted the smaller screens and the change in controls. On BREW, players uncovered their powers as they played, with on-screen hints pointing to possible interactions.[3]

Bight’s CEO Stuart Duncan had spoken earlier that year about building across phone control schemes. Verizon already carried Bight’s Puzzlings, an original iPhone game it had adapted for feature phones.[4]

Release & reception

Capcom Mobile and Bolt Creative announced that Pocket God was available to Verizon feature-phone customers on September 1, 2010. Bolt Creative’s blog identified these as BREW phones. The old carrier link was supplied so players could check whether their handset was supported.[1][3]
