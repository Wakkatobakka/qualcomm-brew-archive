Tetris (240 display)

The Tetris package marked for a 240 display.[1]

In this entry

The gameRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

This copy has the 240 marker in its RAR filename and lists theme.midi. The other Tetris package lists theme.mid. Neither filename gives the full screen dimensions.[1]

Release & reception

JAMDAT and EA put Tetris on their joint mobile line-up when they announced their merger in December 2005. The Tetris Company dates the switch to EA as its exclusive mobile licensee to 2006. MobyGames lists BREW editions under both labels.[2][3][4]
