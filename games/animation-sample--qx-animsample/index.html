Animation Sample

A BREW sample with separate files for a model, a world and five named animations.[1][2]

In this entry

The sampleFiles & versionsSources

The sample

Animation Sample contains files named attack, die, idle, impact and move alongside model and world files. The names make the animation states identifiable without running the sample. The Zeebo Archive records another qx_animsample.rar, a lead for comparing the two source collections.[1][2]
