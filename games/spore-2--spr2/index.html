Spore Creatures

The mobile sequel takes its creature out of the water and turns evolution into a land adventure.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

The preserved descriptor names EA’s Spore Creatures, version 4.1.56 en.lgax265 c. In the separate Java version reviewed by Pocket Gamer, the creature eats plants and smaller animals to evolve, and the DNA system supports exploration instead of the previous game’s creature-sharing codes.[1][2]

People & studios

Electronic Arts

Company named in descriptor (Preserved BREW edition)[1]

Release & reception

Spanner Spencer reviewed the Java release in October 2009. The archived BREW edition keeps a separate version string from that reviewed build.[2][1]
