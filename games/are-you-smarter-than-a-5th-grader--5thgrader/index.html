Are You Smarter Than a 5th Grader?

Capcom and Fox brought the TV classroom quiz to U.S. carriers.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosFiles & versionsSources

The game

Capcom Interactive and Fox announced a U.S. phone version in October 2007. It turned the show’s classroom into a solo quiz with simulated pupils to ask for help. Capcom advertised hundreds of questions and said the title would reach U.S. carriers in the coming weeks.[1][2]

Edition & research notes

This package keeps its questions in fifthqs.dat and its executable under the name fifthgrader.mod. The separately listed 09 copy uses different names for both files. The inventory makes the copies distinguishable even though carrier and handset information has yet to turn up.[3][4]

  • Original carrier store listings, handset targets and BREW-specific credits are still needed.
  • The archives list about.txt and help.txt. Their contents could confirm licensing, release and question-rules details.
