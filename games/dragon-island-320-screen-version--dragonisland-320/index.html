Dragon Island (320-screen version)

Macrospace’s axe-throwing platformer helped launch its BREW lineup in 2003.[1]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

A warrior heads out to slay a dragon, jumping between platforms, grabbing fruit and throwing his axe at the creatures in his way. The fruit collecting and axe throwing reminded GameSpot’s Damon Brown of Hudson’s Adventure Island.[1]

People & studios

Macrospace

Developer (BREW)[2]

Release & reception

In August 2003, Macrospace announced Dragon Island alongside Detonate! and Crash N Burn as its first three premium BREW games. Brown’s 2004 review describes three separate directional jump buttons, with 0 assigned to the axe.[2][1][3]
