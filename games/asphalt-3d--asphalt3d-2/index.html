●Documented game history

Associated credits: Gameloft — original Asphalt mobile series

Qualcomm’s December 2006 announcement promoted Asphalt: Urban GT 2 – 3D as part of a group of Gameloft 3D BREW titles launched through TIM in Italy.

That rollout illustrates how early mobile publishers were beginning to sell polygonal games through individual carrier catalogs, long before a unified smartphone app store.
