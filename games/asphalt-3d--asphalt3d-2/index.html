Asphalt: Urban GT 2 (3D)

Gameloft sold a native 3D edition of Asphalt: Urban GT 2 through Italy’s TIM.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosFiles & versionsSources

The game

At CTIA in September 2005, Gameloft showed GameSpot a sprite-based Asphalt: Urban GT 2 on a Motorola V535. The company was preparing several editions for different hardware. The 3D game reached a documented BREW storefront when Qualcomm announced downloadable games for Italy’s TIM in December 2006.[1][2]

Development

In January 2007, Gameloft counted Asphalt among eight 3D releases it had made for higher-end handsets. The studio planned to keep building versions for less powerful phones, too. It said its wider catalog served roughly 800 handsets across 170 carriers at the time.[3]
