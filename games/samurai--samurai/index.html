Samurai

Ironmonkey’s phone samurai crosses side-scrolling stages to recover the stolen Sunset Blade.[1]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

In JAMDAT’s 2005 Samurai, Toshiro Yamada follows the demon who stole the Sunset Blade. Each stage takes him through side-scrolling fights before a one-on-one boss encounter. GameSpot’s Carrie Gouskos found that the regular fights became manageable once the triple slash was available, but that a single boss could stop the game cold.[1]

Release & reception

GameSpot previewed the JAMDAT game on an LG VX7000 and reviewed it in July 2005. Stephen Palley said combinations on the phone’s directional pad were enough to perform its moves.[2][1]
