Super Drop Mania (176-screen)

A falling-block puzzler with color bombs, preserved here in the 176 × 208 package.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Fill the board with groups of one color, then drop a matching bomb to clear them. The 2005 Series 60 game used Fathammer’s X-Forge 3D engine to rock and rotate the playfield against the backdrop.[1]

Development

Mikko Miettinen designed and programmed the original Drop Mania and is credited as producer on the Symbian adaptation. The contemporary review names both Fathammer and Ninai Games.[2][1]

People & studios

Mikko Miettinen

Producer. Designer and programmer of the original Drop Mania. (Symbian credit roll)[2]

Ninai Games

Fathammer and Ninai Games

Telcogames

Publisher (2007 Java edition and announced US rollout)[4]

Release & reception

Telcogames announced Super Drop Mania for January 2007 when it publicized its expansion into the US BREW market. In March, Pocket Gamer previewed the Java edition and credited Fathammer.[3][4]
