Earthworm Jim

Shiny’s run-and-gun platformer reached Verizon’s BREW store under a Gameloft license.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

An earthworm climbs into a super suit and spends the rest of the game shooting, swinging and making trouble for a collection of equally unlikely villains. Gameloft struck a deal with Interplay in 2009 to bring Earthworm Jim back on phones and downloadable consoles.[1]

Development

In the Java adaptation reviewed by Pocket Gamer, Jim aims at the nearest target on his own. A tap whips, while holding the same button fires his gun. MobyGames credits Gameloft’s Chengdu studio for Java and its Beijing studio for iPhone. The BREW entry credits Shiny for the underlying game and lists Gameloft as publisher.[2][3]

Release & reception

The release record lists the BREW game on Verizon in the U.S. on January 19, 2010, after the iPhone and Java releases in late 2009.[3]
