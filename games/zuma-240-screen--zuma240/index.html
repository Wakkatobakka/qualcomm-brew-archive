Zuma (240-screen)

A frog, a chain of colored marbles and a 240-labelled BREW package.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Shoot matching marbles into the chain to clear them before it reaches the idol. Avery Score reviewed Sorrent’s 2005 adaptation on the LG VX7000, a Verizon Get It Now handset. He used buttons to turn the frog instead of a computer mouse.[1][2]

People & studios

PopCap Games

Original developer (Zuma Deluxe)[3]

Sorrent

Mobile licensee and publisher (2005 handset adaptation)[1]

G-mode

Publisher (Documented Japanese BREW release)[3]

Release & reception

MobyGames lists a separate 2009 Japanese BREW release published by G-mode. The archive’s 240 marker gives no carrier, language or publisher for this copy.[3][4]
