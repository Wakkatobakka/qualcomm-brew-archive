Brothers in Arms (240 display)

Gameloft built a 3D version of Earned in Blood for phones with keypads.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionGalleryFiles & versionsSources

The game

One player reviewing the 3D phone game in May 2006 described five missions in Normandy and three in Tunisia. The control scheme offered automatic targeting or manual aim on the zero key. The slower pace let players work through fights on a keypad, even though the reviewer found movement and frame rate sluggish.[1]

Development

Gameloft made a separate 2D mobile edition of Earned in Blood. In January 2007 the company put the 3D game in a group of eight it had already made for more capable phones. It was still making versions for lower-end handsets while planning a broader 3D slate.[2][3]

Gameloft

TIM

Carrier distribution in Italy (December 2006 BREW storefront)[4]

Release & reception

TIM was selling the game over the air in Italy by Qualcomm’s December 2006 announcement. In June 2007 Qualcomm named Gameloft’s Brothers in Arms 3D its Best Game Application. The award refers to the game rather than either archive’s display label.[4][5]

Edition & research notes

This is the 240-labelled RAR. Both display packages list bia.mod and the same named level, object, texture and sound resources. The archives have different byte sizes. Their names suggest distinct display targets, with exact handset requirements still unconfirmed.[6][7]

  • Full BREW staff credits remain unavailable. The MobyGames credits are for Symbian.
  • The two copies have been distinguished by filename and size. Specific handset, build and carrier metadata remain unconfirmed.
