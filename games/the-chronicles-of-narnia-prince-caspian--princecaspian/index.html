The Chronicles of Narnia: Prince Caspian

Enorbus’s phone adventure put four keys between Prince Caspian and King Miraz.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

In the Java game, Caspian talks to characters and looks for keys and levers. Encounters cut to a close-up sword fight. The 2, 4, 6 and 8 keys handled light attack, block, medium attack and heavy attack, respectively.[1]

Development

In combat, missed swings and incoming hits fill the spirit meter. Once it fills, a companion can join the fight. Keith Andrew found that deliberately taking a hit could end a fight sooner.[2]
