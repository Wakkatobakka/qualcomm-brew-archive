World Poker Tour Hold’em 2

Hands-On’s sequel put phone players at the same tables as people playing in a web browser.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

The Java single-player edition added a Scenario mode with tournaments leading to the US and World Championships. In Free Play, players could set the blinds and choose Limit, Pot Limit or No Limit Hold ’Em.[1]

Development

Stephanie Morgan, a former professional poker player, was project manager at Hands-On. The February 2007 trade-show demo supported avatars, buddy lists and bots that could fill empty seats.[2]

Hands-On Mobile

Release & reception

UK customers on 3 could play the multiplayer Java edition in August 2007. T-Mobile UK added multiplayer in January 2008 after originally selling the solo edition. Players in a browser could join the same tables as phone players.[1][3][4]
