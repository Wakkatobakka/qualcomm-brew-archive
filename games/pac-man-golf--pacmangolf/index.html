Pac-Man Arcade Golf

Namco put arcade cabinets and power-ups on a miniature golf course.[1]

The game

Namco’s PAC-MAN Arcade Golf put working arcade machines on the course. With a coin in hand, you could sink the ball into one, play a short challenge based on Pac-Man, Dig Dug or Galaxian, and start your next shot closer to the hole if you won. The course itself used a two-press power meter, and the number keys let you look around before taking the shot.[1]

Release & reception

Namco Networks announced the game in September 2008. The reporting at launch lists Verizon, AT&T, Sprint, Boost and T-Mobile in the United States. Namco’s product page still documents the controls.[2][1]
