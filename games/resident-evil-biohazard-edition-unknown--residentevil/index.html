Resident Evil: Degeneration

Capcom put Leon Kennedy in a zombie-infested airport and used Ideaworks3D’s Airplay SDK to bring the game to several kinds of phone.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Leon Kennedy fights his way through an airport overrun by zombies in Capcom’s adaptation of the animated film. Capcom’s December announcement describes 50 missions. The preserved BREW package’s handset descriptor gives the title as Resident Evil: Degeneration and the version as 1.00.03.[1][2]

Development

Capcom wanted to ship a 3D game on several native mobile platforms. In October 2008, Ideaworks3D announced that Capcom was using Airplay, its cross-platform SDK and runtime. The BREW package contains both a small phone module and a separate .s3e file, consistent with that development account.[3][2]

Release & reception

Ideaworks3D named KDDI au, Verizon V CAST and Nokia N-Gage in October. In December, Capcom said it expected the game on most UK and North American carriers by the end of 2008. The preserved descriptor gives no carrier for this copy.[3][1][2]
