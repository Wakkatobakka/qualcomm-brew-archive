Need for Speed: Shift

Need for Speed: Shift on BREW, preserved in EA’s 4.4.23 build.[1]

In this entry

The gameFiles & versionsSources

The game

The Java release of Shift had auto-acceleration. To drift, the player pressed 5 entering a bend and held a moving cursor near the middle of an on-screen meter. Pocket Gamer highlighted how much control this gave a player using their thumb.[1]
