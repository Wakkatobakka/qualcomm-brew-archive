HeliStrike

Fishlabs’ helicopter shooter is the strongest title match for this package.[1]

In this entry

The game

In Fishlabs’ Heli Strike 3D, nine missions take the helicopter across oceans, rivers and desert. Campaign medals reward accuracy and efficiency, while Instant Action skips the campaign.[1]

Development

Fishlabs built Heli Strike 3D with its own ABYSS 2.0 engine. The release followed the studio’s space combat game Galaxy on Fire earlier in 2006.[2]

Release & reception

At launch, Superscape handled distribution in the Americas and arvato mobile covered Germany, Austria and Switzerland. A separate October agreement put Heli Strike in I-play’s catalogue for other markets.[2][3]

Edition & research notes

The preserved module is named helistrike.mod, and its companion resource is res_hs_high.aez. Published coverage identifies the mobile shooter, while those filenames leave the identity and distribution history of this build open.[4][1]
