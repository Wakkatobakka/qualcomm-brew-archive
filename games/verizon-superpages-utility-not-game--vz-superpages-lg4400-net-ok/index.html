Verizon SuperPages

Verizon’s directory app, preserved in a package marked for the LG VX4400.[1]

In this entry

The applicationRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The application

Verizon listed Superpages alongside games, messaging and alerts in the 2003 annual report for its Get It Now service. The report describes customers downloading software directly to their phones and paying for individual downloads or subscriptions.[1]

Release & reception

Verizon and LG announced the VX4400 in January 2003. The carrier’s handset sheet lists Get It Now support.[2][3]
