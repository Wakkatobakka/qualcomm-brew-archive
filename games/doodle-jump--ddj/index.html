Doodle Jump

Lima Sky’s game went from pencil sketches to the App Store to Verizon’s BREW catalog.[1][2]

The game

The Doodler bounces automatically. Your job is to guide it onto the next platform and keep climbing as the screen scrolls up.[1][2]

Development

Doodle Jump started as Hop, Bunny, Hop!, a sequel to Lima Sky’s Eat, Bunny, Eat! The brothers had an engine but kept getting stuck on the artwork. When they returned to the project, Igor Pusenjak decided the pencil sketches looked better than the graphics they were trying to replace them with.[1]

On the original iPhone game, Igor Pusenjak imagined a black hole throwing you into another player’s game so they could rescue you. Marko Pusenjak suggested drawing a mark where another player fell so you could pass their score while you climbed. Marko said the first version took about two months to develop in Xcode.[1]

Release & reception

In 2009, Lima Sky agreed to bring Doodle Jump to six more mobile platforms with RealNetworks. MobyGames dates the U.S. BREW release on Verizon to March 16, 2010, a year after the iPhone debut. RealNetworks’ GameHouse label announced a separate Doodle Jump Deluxe for feature phones in June 2011, with four added themes.[2][3][4]

Edition & research notes

The BREW release record credits Lima Sky for development and names RealNetworks as publisher. It names Gamelion and Mr. Goodliving as porting studios only for the Java edition. The preserved package’s release revision and platform-specific credits remain open.[5][3]
