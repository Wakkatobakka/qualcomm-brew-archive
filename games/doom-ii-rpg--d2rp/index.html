Behind the game

RESEARCH & PROVENANCE
●Documented game history

Associated credits: id Mobile — developer; EA Mobile — publisher

DOOM II RPG, developed by id Mobile and distributed by EA Mobile, followed the original DOOM RPG with grid-based movement, turn-based encounters, and a distinctly handheld pace.

Release databases document a BREW edition in December 2009. Unlike a scaled-down PC shooter, this was a purpose-built mobile interpretation of DOOM.
