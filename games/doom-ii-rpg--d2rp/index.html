DOOM II RPG

The second DOOM phone RPG puts a lunar base, three playable characters and turn-based fights on a keypad.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

A distress call draws a team to the UAC base on the Moon. You choose one of three characters and move through the base in first person. Enemies take their turns after you act, giving you time to choose a weapon or work the surroundings to your advantage.[1]

Development

Mobile programmer Jacob Enget worked on DOOM II RPG as part of a six-person team. He puts its multiplatform production at ten months. The low-end engine had a 300 KB memory limit. Enget says he halved the memory needed for images and brought total memory use down by a third to make the game fit.[2]

Release & reception

id Software announced the sequel at QuakeCon in 2008. The release record puts the U.S. BREW launch on Verizon on December 21, 2009, under EA Mobile. The iPhone edition followed in February 2010 under id Software’s own publishing label.[3][4]
