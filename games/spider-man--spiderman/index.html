Ultimate Spider-Man

Mforma’s phone adaptation sends Spider-Man and Venom after each other through the same New York streets.[1]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Choose Spider-Man or Venom and track the other character through streets, offices and construction sites. Spider-Man has to hurry before Venom eats too many people. As Venom, eating civilians buys him time to find Spider-Man. Both can climb and cross parts of the city the people below can’t reach.[1]

People & studios

MFORMA

Developer and publisher (Named phone adaptation)[1]

Release & reception

Stephen Palley reviewed Mforma’s phone adaptation in October 2005. He found the controls responsive, but the chase lost its novelty once he found a reliable way to beat his rival.[1]
