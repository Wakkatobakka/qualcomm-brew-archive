Need for Speed: Hot Pursuit on BREW, preserved in EA’s 4.1.79 build.[1]

The gameFiles & versionsSources

The game

Hot Pursuit’s Java edition split its attention between driving a sports car and chasing racers as the police. Keith Andrew enjoyed the races but found that boosting away from the police rarely worked. In the chases he tested, progress came from damaging the pursuing car.[1]
