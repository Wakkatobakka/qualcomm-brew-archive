Associated credits: MachineWorks Northwest — documented related BREW mobile game

Multiple original Duke Nukem Mobile productions reached BREW in the mid-2000s. MachineWorks Northwest worked on mobile editions, adapting the franchise for very different memory, screen, and control limitations.

The archive calls this package DukeNukem3D_114.rar. Its version label alone is not enough to establish a one-to-one match with the 1996 PC game or a particular marketing title.
