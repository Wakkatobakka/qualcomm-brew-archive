Duke Nukem 3D

MachineWorks put Duke’s first-person adventure on Verizon’s V CAST phones, with two ways to draw its enemies.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Duke heads from the city streets into mansions and dungeons and winds up on a zeppelin. MachineWorks Northwest built the BREW shooter around the structure of its earlier Tapwave Zodiac game and expanded the stages.[1][2]

Development

On the LG VX8000 that GameSpot tried at BREW 2005, the preview ran at about 15 frames per second with polygonal enemies. Switching to flat sprites raised that to about 20. The preserved package lists several level resources alongside files with the same names ending in _spr.[1][3]

Release & reception

MobyGames dates the Verizon V CAST release to September 11, 2005. Avery Score liked the phone’s graphics and firefights in GameSpot’s review, but found that turning around on the keypad could take too long. An optional quick-look control sped it up.[4][1][5]

Edition & research notes

The main descriptor says Duke Nukem 3D, v.1.1.4., and MachineWorks Northwest. A separate descriptor names HI Corporation and version 3.0.0 C. The carrier and handset for this package remain open.[3]
