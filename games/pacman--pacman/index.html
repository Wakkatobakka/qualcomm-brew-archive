Pac-Man

Namco’s maze chase arrived on North American feature phones under the original Pac-Man name.[1]

In this entry

The gameRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Namco’s phone catalogue describes the familiar maze game. Eat the dots to advance, grab fruit for points and use the power pellets to turn the tables on the ghosts. Namco supplied a separate phone user guide and listed compatible handsets by carrier.[1]

Release & reception

By March 2005, Namco was describing PAC-MAN as one of its biggest-selling phone games. Its official phone manual explains how score bonuses and the temporary power-pellet effect worked.[2][3]
