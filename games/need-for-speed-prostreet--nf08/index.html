Need for Speed: ProStreet

EA’s phone version put tuning, nitrous and drifting on the keypad.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

The mobile ProStreet offered sixteen licensed cars, road circuits and a tuner shop. In the Java version reviewed by Pocket Gamer, drifting supplied much of the pleasure. Its handling made the game feel fast even through a phone thumbstick.[1][2]

People & studios

EA Mobile

Publisher (Announced phone release)[1]

IronMonkey Studios

Developer (Related J2ME adaptation, community attribution)[4]

Release & reception

EA Mobile announced October 26, 2007 for Europe and October 30 for North America, with distribution through most major carriers. This package’s nfsps module supports the ProStreet identification. Its precise carrier and build remain open.[1][3]
