Street Fighter II: Champion Edition

Capcom’s arcade revision brought twelve fighters to phones, with different editions on different carriers.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Champion Edition opened up the four boss characters from Street Fighter II and revised the returning fighters. In Japan, Capcom used the arcade name Street Fighter II Dash for its EZweb edition.[1][2]

People & studios

Capcom

Publisher (Documented Japanese EZweb BREW edition)[1]

Release & reception

ITmedia reported that Capcom began selling the game through Club Capcom on June 15, 2006. It cost ¥525 to download and required a supported BREW 3.1 handset, including the W41CA, W41T and neon. The report includes a contemporary Capcom image.[1]

Pocket Gamer reviewed a separate English-language Java edition in August 2007. Chris Pickering found that it offered a choice between simplified special moves and entering the original combinations on the keypad.[3]
