Asteroids

Atari’s rotating ship and splitting rocks returned in several phone editions.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameFiles & versionsSources

The game

Glu’s 2007 Asteroids gave players a choice: play with the original rules in Classic mode or use new themes, weapons and power-ups. The company advertised a health meter for its expanded modes. Its launch announcement gives a reference point for comparing one of the phone versions against this copy.[1][2]

Edition & research notes

This package has four files, including the executable and highscore.dat. Its inventory identifies the title without tying it to Glu’s particular edition.[3]

  • The included highscore.dat and Asteroids resource names do not identify the publisher. The game’s title and credits screens or strings would help distinguish the feature-phone editions.
  • The archive has no carrier or handset ID confirmed for this file.
