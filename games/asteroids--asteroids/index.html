Associated credits: Atari — original arcade game

Atari launched Asteroids in arcades in 1979. Its rotating ship, wraparound playfield, and fragmented space rocks became a foundational action-game design.

This BREW archive documents a later phone copy, not the original arcade binary or a verified licensee of this exact port.
