The sample

The source inventory calls this a Projected Shadow QX demo. The instructions, author and SDK version for this copy are unverified.[1]

Development

Qualcomm announced its QX Engine SDK at BREW 2006. It let developers prototype 3D models and animations with hardware-accelerated OpenGL ES on Qualcomm chipsets. Qualcomm’s 2009 developer slides show the modeling exporters, rendering engine and texture tools. The specific relationship between this archived demo and the SDK is still unverified.[2][3]
