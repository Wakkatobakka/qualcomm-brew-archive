Call of Duty

MFORMA’s first Call of Duty for phones had players pick three soldiers before each mission.[1][2]

The game

The 2004 phone game asked you to pick three soldiers before each mission. Keith Stuart described ordering them through a war zone and using their individual skills, such as sniping or setting explosives. MFORMA’s 2006 announcement called Call of Duty 2 a sequel to that game.[1][2]

Edition & research notes

The archive keeps its executable and main resources under the Call of Duty name. None of the listed filenames carries a publisher or credits version, leaving the relationship to the reviewed MFORMA edition open.[3]

  • Package credits, release date, carrier and handset are unconfirmed. The Guardian review and MFORMA’s 2006 announcement identify the related 2004 game.
