The game

The Run’s 2011 Java edition ties a sequence of races and speed challenges together with a story. Pocket Gamer’s Keith Andrew found that nitrous and slow motion often decided his results. A separate iOS version was cancelled that December when EA moved resources to another mobile project.[1][2]
