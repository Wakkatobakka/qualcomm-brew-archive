Who Wants to Be a Millionaire? (2009)

The 2009 edition of Millionaire was already on Verizon phones in May 2008.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosFiles & versionsSources

The game

Capcom built its 2009 edition around the US show hosted by Meredith Vieira. It launched exclusively on Verizon Wireless and was among the carrier’s top-selling mobile games that May, according to Capcom.[1]

Development

Capcom bought Cosmic Infinity, the Canadian studio that held the North American mobile rights, in 2006. By June 2008, Capcom said the licensed series had sold three million downloads across editions dating back to 2003.[2][3]
