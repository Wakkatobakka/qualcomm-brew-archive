Capcom Interactive announced that Who Wants to Be a Millionaire 2009 had launched through Verizon Wireless in 2008, and was among the carrier’s best-selling phone games that May.

Capcom’s licensed mobile series had already sold millions of downloads, making this more than a novelty TV tie-in.
