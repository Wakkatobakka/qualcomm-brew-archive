Wild 8-Ball (v1.08)

Sorrent crossed pool with poker for Verizon’s early BREW games store.[1]

The game

Qualcomm and Sorrent described Wild 8-Ball as a pool-and-poker puzzle and reported it was already available on Verizon’s Get It Now by February 2003.[1]
