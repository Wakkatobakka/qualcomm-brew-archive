Are You Smarter Than a 5th Grader? (2009)

The collection’s second 5th Grader package carries the 09 edition label.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Both quiz packages include separate question, help and about files. In this one the question file is fifthqs09.dat and the executable is fifthbrew.mod. Those names distinguish it from the unnumbered archive.[1][2]

Release & reception

Capcom and Fox also used the 2009 title for an iPhone and iPod touch edition announced on September 2, 2008. Capcom described the Apple version as a fresh build with new questions and animations. That release date belongs to the Apple edition. An original carrier listing for the BREW edition is still needed.[3]
