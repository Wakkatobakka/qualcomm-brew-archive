WeatherBug

Verizon offered local WeatherBug reports by text, through Get It Now and in its mobile browser.[1][2]

The application

Verizon made WeatherBug its weather partner for several services in 2007. The agreement put neighborhood conditions in Get It Now, SMS, V CAST Video and Mobile Web 2.0.[1][2]
