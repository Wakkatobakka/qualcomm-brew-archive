Associated credits: Gameloft Software Chengdu — documented mobile developer

A separate mobile adaptation of Tom Clancy’s H.A.W.X. appeared in 2009, distinct from the console and PC release. The recorded mobile developer is Gameloft’s Chengdu studio.

The handset version used a different style of aerial action to accommodate mobile controls; the archive does not identify the original handset for this copy.

Contemporary or historical source covers this named game; the exact archive revision is not independently verified.
