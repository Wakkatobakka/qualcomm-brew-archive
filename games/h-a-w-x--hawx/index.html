H.A.W.X.

Gameloft’s feature-phone H.A.W.X. sends fighter jets through a top-down shooter.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

In the documented handset adaptation, the plane fires by itself so you can steer through waves of enemies and collect power-ups. The Java edition reserves * for missiles and 0 for a smart bomb. Between battles, canyon runs send you after a trail of arrows.[1][2]

People & studios

Gameloft Software Chengdu

Developer (Documented feature-phone edition)[2]

Release & reception

Gameloft’s Chengdu studio is credited for the feature-phone game, and Pocket Gamer reviewed the Java release in March 2009. Jon Mundy admired the detail in the backgrounds, but the buildup of scenery, enemies and power-ups made the player’s plane hard to see.[2][1]
