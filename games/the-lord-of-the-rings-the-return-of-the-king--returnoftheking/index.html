The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King

The BREW adventure sent Aragorn into the Paths of the Dead and put his scores on a Verizon leaderboard.[1]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Aragorn fights the King of the Dead. In Minas Tirith, the player can face the orcs as Gandalf or Pippin. GameSpot’s 2004 review describes a side-scrolling action RPG whose stages could be replayed with a different character and scored separately.[1]

People & studios

JAMDAT Mobile

Publisher and credited studio (Reviewed BREW edition)[1]

ImaginEngine

Credited studio (Reviewed BREW edition)[1]

Release & reception

GameSpot credits JAMDAT and ImaginEngine on the BREW edition. Its review points readers to Verizon’s Battle for Middle-earth Tournament and reports that players could upload a separate score for each stage.[1][2]
