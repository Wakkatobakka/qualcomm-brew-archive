Associated credits: Magellan Interactive — developer; JAMDAT Mobile — publisher

The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King had a distinct mobile edition released in 2003. MobyGames credits Magellan Interactive with development and JAMDAT Mobile with publishing its BREW version.

It belongs to the first wave of blockbuster film adaptations that mobile carriers sold as small downloadable games.

Contemporary or historical source covers this named game; the exact archive revision is not independently verified.
