The Flintstones

Macrospace made two Flintstones phone games. This package’s filename gives neither subtitle.[1][2]

In this entry

The gamePeople & studiosFiles & versionsSources

The game

In Grocery Hunt, Fred and Barney bring home shopping bags with holes in them and have until nightfall to retrace their steps. Macrospace also made The Flintstones Bedrock Bowling. Go Mobile in Malta offered both as Java downloads in 2004, at Lm2.50 per game plus the data charge.[1][2]
