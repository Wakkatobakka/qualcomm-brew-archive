Centipede

Centipede appeared on Sprint Vision in 2004 and later returned in Glu’s Atari lineup.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameFiles & versionsSources

The game

GameSpot reviewed Centipede on Sprint Vision in 2004 and found that it kept the arcade game’s mushroom field, spiders and segmented enemies. Glu later launched another phone adaptation in early 2007 under its Atari agreement. The two releases give useful comparisons for the copy preserved here.[1][2]
