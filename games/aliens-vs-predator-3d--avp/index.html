Aliens vs. Predator 3D

Superscape Moscow built a first-person rail shooter around the limits of a phone keypad.[1]

In this entry

The game

The phone moves you through each level while you line up shots. Superscape Moscow used that arcade rail-shooter format for the 3D game, its second adaptation of the film after a 2D platform adventure. The team designed five levels and three modes, with timed and survival play unlocked through the story.[1]

Development

Gregory Herboek, Superscape Moscow’s creative director, said the team sketched every level and planned enemy placements in a spreadsheet. That let the designers control the difficulty from the opening encounters through the final fight with the Alien Queen. Fox supplied reference material and signed off at each milestone. After the team reached release candidate, it tested against operators’ rules and ported the game to multiple devices.[1]

Herboek said the aiming system was designed to spend less time lining up targets. The build could play two or three sounds together and change the audio in response to the action. Predators carried different weapons, and alien warriors could jump from walls and ceilings.[1]

Release & reception

In April 2006 Herboek told readers to look in their operator’s game catalog in the United States. MobyGames dates the BREW release to 2006. Superscape later made a separate mobile game based on AvP: Requiem.[1][2][3]
