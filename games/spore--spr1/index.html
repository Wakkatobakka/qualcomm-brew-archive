Spore

The internal name is spore. Babaroga’s Spore Origins is a documented BREW release.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Spore Origins started on BREW. Babaroga’s phone game used the microorganism stage of Spore as its setting. EA later had the game rebuilt for iPhone, where the team experimented with tilt controls and lengthened the campaign.[1][2]

Development

At GDC Mobile in 2009, EA Mobile producer Mike Pagano described how the iPhone team started from the BREW game and explored different controls before choosing tilt. He put the earlier playtime at around two hours and the expanded iPhone game at around five.[1]

EA Mobile’s Scott Humphries described the phone game as an adaptation of Spore’s cell stage. The creature editor was part of the plan even for a release spanning hundreds of feature phones and smartphones. Steve Santello was one of Babaroga’s artists on the mobile project and later wrote about how the team designed its interface.[3][4]

Release & reception

AT&T advertised Spore Origins as an exclusive for its subscribers through early September 2008. MobyGames separately records a September 2008 BREW release under the EA Mobile label and names Babaroga as its developer.[5][2]
