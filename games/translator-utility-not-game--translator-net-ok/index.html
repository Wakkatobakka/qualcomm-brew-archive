Edition & research notes

For a separate example from the same era, Transclick’s developers published a 2005 usability study of a BREW translator on a Verizon Motorola phone. They used a remote server and studied how keypad entry, scrolling and translation delays affected users.[2]

  • Link Mobile Solutions appears in the handset descriptor as the company behind Tradutor. Find the original listing and supported languages.
