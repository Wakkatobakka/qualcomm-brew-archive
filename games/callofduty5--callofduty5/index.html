Call of Duty: World at War

Glu’s phone adaptation follows the war across the Pacific and Europe.[1]

The game

The preserved module uses the name cod5. Its handset descriptor names it Call of Duty: WaW and credits Glu Mobile.[1]

People & studios

Glu Mobile

Named mobile publisher (Preserved BREW edition)[1]

Release & reception

Glu announced its phone adaptation in June 2008, describing missions in the Pacific and European theaters.[2][3]
