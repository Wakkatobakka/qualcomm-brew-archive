The Dark Knight

On Java phones, Batman’s punch, glide and grappling hook shared a single key.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

The 5 key could punch a nearby enemy, glide into an opponent farther away, or send Batman swinging to another roof. Pocket Gamer’s reviewer found that a fight could take over the controls just when he wanted to grapple out of it.[1]

Development

Glu showed a nearly complete game in June 2008. Its preview build included the Mangler, a powered fist that could break open a fence or send a henchman flying. Glu’s Justin Kubiak also described how the Joker would reappear through the story.[2]
