Jewel Quest

Big Blue Bubble adapted iWin’s browser puzzle for phones in the mid-2000s.[1]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosFiles & versionsSources

The game

Rearranging the relics turns the sand tiles underneath them to gold. GameFAQs’ description of the 2005 phone edition mentions cursed items and buried artifacts.[1]

Development

Studio founder Damir Slogar recalled that iWin supplied the game, I-play picked up the licence and Big Blue Bubble built the phone adaptation. He counted more than two million downloads across the mobile Jewel Quest series in 2007.[2]
