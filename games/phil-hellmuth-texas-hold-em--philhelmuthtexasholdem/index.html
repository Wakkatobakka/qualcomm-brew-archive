Phil Hellmuth Texas Hold’em

Summus put the World Series of Poker champion’s name on a real-time phone poker game.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionFiles & versionsSources

The game

Summus licensed Phil Hellmuth’s name for its mobile poker game in 2004. You could set up a table with up to seven computer opponents, play a no-limit match against a tougher computer version of Hellmuth, or join an online table.[1][2]

Development

Summus’s game combined three types of online tables with a timer for each move. In single-player games, you could slow the action down and choose how many opponents to face. For GameSpot, the computer players’ readiness to bluff and to raise after checking made them more interesting than the interface suggested.[2]

Release & reception

Summus dates its initial release to May 2004. The company, by then called Oasys Mobile, reported in its next annual filing that the game had passed 750,000 users by December 2005. The filing counts the game across carriers and handset platforms.[1][3]
