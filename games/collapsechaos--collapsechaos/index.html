RealNetworks brought its matching-block puzzle to BREW. This copy identifies itself as version 1.0.1 x.[1][2]

The game

Collapse! Chaos has players remove groups of matching blocks as the board fills up. MobyGames records a 2008 BREW release. The preserved descriptor names RealNetworks and Collapse! Chaos.[1][2]
