Behind the game

RESEARCH & PROVENANCE
●Documented game history

Associated credits: IG Fun — documented mobile adaptation

At BREW 2008, IG Fun demonstrated a real-time, fully 3D mobile adaptation of BioShock. Contemporary reporting described a BREW handset rendering the action rather than simply playing a prerecorded animation.

The company also worked on a different 2D BioShock edition for less powerful handsets. Both existed because mobile chips, screen resolutions, and carrier requirements varied so dramatically.
