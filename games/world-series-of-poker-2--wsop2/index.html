World Series of Poker 2: Pro Challenge

Glu’s Pro Challenge put Johnny Chan and Annie Duke across the table and gave each opponent a Tilt Meter.[1][2]

The game

In the Java edition, repeated losses fill an opponent’s Tilt Meter. Once rattled, a player can make mistakes or give away a tell. The opponents included real players Chris Ferguson, Annie Duke and Johnny Chan, plus actor and poker player Shannon Elizabeth.[1][2]

People & studios

Glu Mobile

Exclusive mobile licensee and publisher (Documented World Series of Poker mobile games)[6]

Release & reception

Glu lists Pro Challenge as a second-quarter 2007 release, the follow-up to its 2006 mobile Texas Hold’em game. The Windows Mobile release is catalogued under the longer name World Series of Poker 2: Pro Challenge.[3][1][4]
