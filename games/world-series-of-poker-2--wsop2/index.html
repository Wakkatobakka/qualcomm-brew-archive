World Series of Poker 2 was preserved here as an independently named phone package, within the era when licensed poker products were sold through carrier storefronts.

Its name suggests the tournament license, but this entry deliberately avoids confusing it with a later console World Series of Poker title or borrowing the credits of another mobile poker game.
