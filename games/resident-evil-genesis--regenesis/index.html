Resident Evil: Genesis

Capcom returned to the Spencer mansion for a phone game built around short missions and puzzles.[1][2]

In this entry

The gameDevelopmentPeople & studiosRelease & receptionGalleryFiles & versionsSources

The game

Genesis takes place in a mansion infested with zombies. You search rooms, piece together clues and scavenge weapons as a surviving S.T.A.R.S. officer. The puzzles carry you from one short mission to the next.[1][2]

Development

Capcom introduced Genesis as a new mobile adventure based on the events of the original Resident Evil. Its product description makes a specific accommodation for playing on a phone: the missions let players save after short stretches.[1][2]

People & studios

Capcom Interactive

Publisher (North American mobile release)[1]

Release & reception

Capcom announced North American availability on March 12, 2008 and said most major carriers were already offering it. The publisher’s announcement reported an A from 1UP and a 7.8 out of 10 from IGN.[1]

Capcom also published a press album with three screenshots labelled for Genesis, showing gameplay from the mobile edition. The album does not name the handset used for the images.[3]
