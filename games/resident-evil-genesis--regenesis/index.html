Behind the game

RESEARCH & PROVENANCE

Associated credits: Capcom Interactive — mobile publisher

Resident Evil: Genesis was published by Capcom Interactive in March 2008. Instead of attempting a one-to-one port of the PlayStation game, it returned to the mansion setting in a mobile-friendly format emphasizing exploration and puzzles.

Capcom’s own announcement describes Jill’s survival adventure and calls it a newly created mobile game.
