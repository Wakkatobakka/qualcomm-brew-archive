A LITTLE LOST HISTORY

There was more to phone gaming than Java.

Before app stores became the default, Qualcomm’s Binary Runtime Environment for Wireless (BREW) helped bring downloadable games and applications to feature phones.

This site indexes one surviving collection from that world. It is a catalog of packages, not 104 confirmed distinct retail games: some are different screen versions, demos, utilities or add-ons.
